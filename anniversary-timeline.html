The First Trip Together

A spontaneous road trip sealed the deal. They laughed through a broken car heater, navigated without GPS, and arrived somewhere far better than their destination — completely, irreversibly in love.

1995

The Wedding Day

June 14th, 1995. Surrounded by family and the scent of garden roses, they said "I do" under a sky that seemed to hold its breath. The day began a promise that has only grown stronger.

June 14, 1995
1998

Their First Home

A fixer-upper on Elmwood Drive with creaky floors and a garden that would take years to tame. They painted every room themselves, arguing cheerfully about every shade. It became the warmest place any of us have ever known.

A new chapter
2001

A Family Grows

The patter of small feet changed everything — and somehow made them love each other even more deeply. Sleepless nights, first words, tiny hands. The adventure had new protagonists.

Family
2010

The 15th Anniversary

They returned to the same restaurant where they had their very first date. Same table, same view — but a lifetime of shared stories between them. The waiter mistook them for newlyweds.

Halfway there
2018

Dream Trip to Italy

The trip they'd talked about for twenty years finally happened. Cobblestones, gelato, golden-hour light over the Amalfi Coast. They danced — badly and happily — in a piazza while strangers applauded.

Adventure revisited
2025

Thirty Beautiful Years

Three decades. Countless shared mornings. Hands still reaching for each other across the table. Their love story is not a single great moment — it is thousands of small ones, strung together like lights on a very long and very wonderful night.
